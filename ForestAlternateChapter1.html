Through the mirror

Volume 1 - Chapter 1

You wake up at unfamiliar interior place. The walls, the ceilings, and the floors were made of an old oak wood as if being stuck inside of a box. You see no furnitures, windows, and doors at their place. Aside a humanoid figure holding an bloody shovel at its side standing in front of their fireplace. It wears a white mask with eye sockets.

While you're observing the being. Suddenly realized that your hands and legs are being tied up on the ground. Trying to get yourself free from this unknown situations. Struggling.

You look at the being again.

Instantly, the humanoid figure cracks their head to turn their side. Looking at you in the eye.

"You're awake." Said the figure as he goes towards you by steps while drops something unto the fire. "Cold isn't? Too cold for living the same boat?".

You were confused what was the man talks about.

"Don't hide from me." The voice of man angered at you. Looking at you with his fading light through his eye sockets. His breathing were shown cold as clouds within his mask.

He raised his bloody shovel trembling into the air. " I know you're there. I. Know. You're. There. "

The room slowly gets darker, and darker while you're struggling to set yourself free from this unknown of somewhat paranormal activity. You tried to call for help, but no one's there except the man whose about to swing his blunt weapon at any moment in so-called room.

"If only IT were to be selfish as you and I. Things could have changed. One day, WE would've been so.. nice to have the same ideals."

"But YOU refused." The man continues his talk while he gripped his hold on unto the shovel in the air.
